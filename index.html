Arrow Puzzle: World Tour · Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Arrow Puzzle: World Tour ("the game") is made by Pixaru. This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location. You do not need an account to play.

Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, settings) is saved only on your device. We do not receive it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect:

  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location
  • how you interact with ads and the app (for example app launches, taps and ad views)
  • diagnostic information such as app performance and crash data

This data is sent to Google and encrypted in transit. Google's use of it is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and by How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

3. Your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice in the game's Settings.
  • On any Android device you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings > Google > Ads (the name can differ by phone).
  • Uninstalling the game deletes all game data stored on your device.

4. Children

The game is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

5. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with Google AdMob as described above.

6. Security and retention

We do not run our own servers for the game, so we do not store your data. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's policies.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
