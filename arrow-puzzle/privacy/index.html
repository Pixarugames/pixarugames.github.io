01Who we are

Arrow Puzzle: World Tour ("the game") is made by Pixaru, an independent game developer. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. You do not need an account to play.

02Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, power-ups and settings) is saved only on your device. We do not receive it and we do not run servers that store it.

If you email us, we receive your email address and message, and use them only to reply.

03Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect:

  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location (country or region)
  • how you interact with ads and the app, such as app launches, taps and ad views
  • diagnostic information such as app performance and crash data

This data is sent directly to Google and encrypted in transit. Google's use of it is described in the Google Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

04How information is used

  • To show ads, including rewarded ads you choose to watch, and to limit how often ads appear
  • To measure ad performance and prevent fraud
  • To keep the game working and fix problems

For players in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, personalised ads are shown only with your consent. Non-personalised ads rely on legitimate interests, such as fraud prevention and keeping the game free.

05Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with Google AdMob as described above. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

06Your choices and rights

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy settings.
  • Advertising ID (all Android devices): you can reset or delete it in your phone's Settings > Google > Ads (names can differ by phone). Without it, ads are not personalised.
  • Europe and the UK (GDPR): you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your data, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.
  • United States: depending on your state, you may have the right to know, delete or correct personal information and to opt out of targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information.

Because we do not hold your data on our own servers, most requests are handled by changing the settings above or by contacting Google. Email us and we will help.

07Children

The game is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

08Retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the game, which deletes it. Emails to us are kept only as long as needed to help you. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's policies.

09International transfers

Google may process data in countries other than your own. Google uses safeguards such as standard contractual clauses for these transfers.

10Security

Ad data is encrypted in transit. We take reasonable steps to protect the game, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

11Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above. Important changes will also be noted in the game's update notes.
